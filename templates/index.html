LOKI POLYMARKET BOT SIMULATION

A disciplined bot that writes its exits first.

The bot prices every open Polymarket price ladder (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Nvidia…) from the live spot price and recent volatility, blends that half-and-half with the crowd, and walks the real order book. It buys the YES or NO token only when fair value beats the price it would actually pay by 3+ points after fees, sizes with quarter-Kelly, and checks every position's exits each minute: out 30 minutes before the market locks, a 40% stop-loss, take-profit, or when the edge is gone.

    PREDICTIVE ARBITRAGE ENGINE PAPER

    Spot moves first. Does the book catch up?

    Every second, on your device: Binance 1-second prices price Polymarket's 5-minute "Up or Down" contract exactly the way it settles (Chainlink's 60-second average at the end vs the start price), then compare with both live order books after the market's own fee. A signal waits the measured network delay and re-reads the book before a paper fill, so you see how much edge survives. Never sends a real order; real latency arbitrage is a race against co-located bots.

        LIQUIDITY REWARDS PAPER

        Earn the spread, not the bet.

        Polymarket pays daily rewards to traders who keep two-sided quotes close to the middle of rewarded markets. Every 5 seconds this simulator reads the real order book, posts a paper bid and ask inside the reward band and leans them against its inventory. Fills come only from real trade prints that crossed its price (a print exactly at its price counts half, since a new quote waits in line), and its share of the reward pool follows Polymarket's published scoring. The risk is real too: when news moves the market, the side that fills is usually the wrong one.

          RED TEAM LAB · PAPER

          Every trade has to survive an attack first.

          No trade is approved because one model liked it. A Quant agent argues for it, a Risk Manager with an absolute veto sizes it, an Execution engine picks the order type, a Red Team throws flash crashes, empty order books, latency spikes and news-spread blowouts at it, and a Devil's Advocate argues the opposite trade. A 2/3 supermajority of five jurors is needed. The jurors are deterministic statistical agents running in your browser, not chatbots: the same data always gives the same verdict, and every dissent is written down.

          02 · LOCAL PAPER BOOK PAPER

          Zero-dependency fills.

          03 · TILT DETECTOR & CIRCUIT BREAKERS

          Guardrails against yourself.

          Three max-loss trades in a row lock a strategy out for 24 hours. Size shrinks as the book draws down (100% at the peak to 25% at the limit). A strategy with no positive risk-adjusted return over 30 days goes to quarantine. Manual trades against the plan are logged as drift. Automated entries pause 15 minutes before high-impact releases.

          04 · LOCAL REPLAY ENGINE

          Replay the fast markets, offline.
